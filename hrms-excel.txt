1: Process | Step 1 | Step 2 | Step 3 | Step 4 | Final Result
2: Recruitment | HR creates vacancy | Candidate shortlisted | Interview/selection | Offer & documents | Candidate converted to Employee
3: Joining | Documents collected | Offer/appointment issued | Onboarding checklist | Employee ID created | Active Employee
4: Leave | Employee applies | Manager approves | HR approval if required | Balance updated | Attendance updated
5: Attendance Correction | Employee requests | Manager checks | HR approves if required | Record updated | Correct attendance
6: Payroll | Attendance locked | LOP/incentive calculated | Payroll processed | HR/Accounts review | Payslip + payroll reports
7: Expense | Employee submits | Manager approves | Accounts verifies | Payment processed | Reimbursement closed
8: Exit | Resignation submitted | Manager/HR approval | Notice & clearance | F&F processed | Relieving + Experience Certificate
